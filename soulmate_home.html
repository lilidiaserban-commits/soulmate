FAQ

Good to know

Is this real / accurate?

It's for entertainment and self-reflection, a creative interpretation made just for you, not a prediction, psychic service, or advice of any kind. Enjoy it in that spirit. 💛

What exactly do I get?

A personalized written reading plus an AI-generated portrait, delivered instantly on this website and to your email. Some readings offer optional extras (like a keepsake PDF or a deeper report).

How fast is it?

Seconds. Right after you finish the quiz, your reading and portrait are generated and shown to you, and a copy is emailed to you.

It's a one-time purchase starting at €15, with a few optional add-ons you can choose. See the pricing page for details.

What about my data & refunds?

We keep only what's needed to create and send your reading, and we never sell your data, see our Privacy Policy. If something goes wrong technically, contact us and we'll make it right, see our Refund Policy.
